The Evolution

A DECADE OF
MASTER CRAFT.

What began as a connoisseur's salon for rare mechanical complications has evolved into a premier atelier celebrating both independent horologists and master fine jewelers. Every artifact we present is engineered for generational permanence.

2016 / Foundation

The Geneva Collectors Salon

Established in Geneva as a private guild for horology purists, archiving and curating limited-run calibers from independent Swiss and French microbrands.

2020 / Joaillerie Expansion

The Haute Joaillerie Atelier

Welcomed master goldsmiths and certified gemologists into the maison. Introduced ethical diamond grading, bespoke engagement suites, and rare Colombian emerald commissions.

2026 / Present Day

Vermeil & Co. Global Prestige

Today, Vermeil & Co. serves an international clientele across 24 countries, delivering certified GIA natural diamonds, fair-mined 18k solid gold, and hand-calibrated mechanical timepieces.

Geneva Goldsmith Bench • Microscopic Diamond Setting
Artisanal Mastery

THE SOUL OF THE GOLDSMITH.

At Vermeil & Co., jewelry is not mass-manufactured on assembly lines. Each ring, bracelet, and pendant begins with an original hand-drawn gouache sketch followed by wax carving and precision lost-wax casting in solid 18k yellow, white, and rose gold.

Our master diamond setters work with high-power binocular loupes, seating every gemstone with microscopic prongs to ensure maximum light performance and unbreakable security. We honor centuries-old European goldsmith traditions, complemented by laser-welding tolerances.

Gemological Integrity

ETHICAL GEMOLOGY & SOURCING

Vermeil & Co. enforces uncompromising ethical standards across all diamonds, colored gemstones, and precious metals.

The Kimberley Accord

Conflict-Free Diamonds

Every solitaire above 0.50 carats is accompanied by an individual GIA dossier certifying D-F colorless grades, ideal proportions, and non-conflict Canadian and African origin.

Oceanic Rarity

South Sea & Akoya Pearls

Hand-harvested from protected lagoons. Each lustrous sphere features thick nacre coatings and mirrors glowing undertones, individually hand-knotted on pure silk cords.

Fair-Mined Protocols

Recycled 18K Solid Gold

We alloy our own 18k yellow, rose, and white gold using certified recycled bullion. Precision solid-link curb chains and box clasps resist tarnish across generations.

Chromatic Phenomenon

Lightning Ridge Opals

Natural untreated black and crystal opals featuring electrifying play-of-color. Sourced directly from Australian miners under ethical provenance standards.

Eternal Vows

Artisanal Wedding Bands

Comfort-fit ergonomic profiles cast in dense 950 platinum and 18k gold. Hand-burnished satin finishes and channel-set eternity diamonds celebrate lasting bonds.
