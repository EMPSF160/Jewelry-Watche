The Standards of Excellence

Every diamond and colored gemstone in the Vermeil & Co. collection exceeds internationally recognized gemological criteria.

01.

Supreme Cut

Only 'Excellent' and 'Ideal' cut grades with perfect symmetry, maximizing internal light refraction and fire.

02.

Colorless Grade

We curate diamonds strictly in the D through F range—representing the pinnacle of natural, icy-white rarity.

03.

VVS1+ Clarity

Free from eye-visible inclusions. Stones undergo 10x binocular inspection to guarantee immaculate crystalline structure.

04.

Ethical Sourcing

100% Kimberley Process certified, conflict-free, and cast with fair-mined recycled precious metals.
